# DBV Unreal AI v0.5.0 — Release notes

**[🇪🇸 Español](./v0.5.0.md) · 🇬🇧 English** · 2026-11-10

Version v0.5.0: **black box** to debug what happens while you play (record the game without touching it, mark moments with a key, analyse with data and know when to trust it); **dressing MetaHumans** from the app with a deterministic tool, garment types, Chaos Cloth and validation (tight shirts and trousers stay experimental); and fixes, including `unreal_playtest` now starting Play on UE 5.8.2 and a security one (a session name cannot escape its folder). Left for v0.5.1: the VR headset, isolating and comparing sessions and the graphics memory report.

### Known issues
- **Tight tops and pants on MetaHumans (REQ-98): experimental.** The body-shell garment with draping measures well (no vertices inside the skin) but does not look presentable (crumpled cloth and texture baked over the anatomy). It does not block v0.5.0; loose garments, skirts and dresses work. To be revisited in v0.6.0 with MetaHuman-ready garments and Chaos Outfit.

### Known (Important, `/code-simplify` review of 2026-11-10)
- The Python recorder catches `Exception` at the boundaries with the Unreal API (which raises a bare `Exception`, and an error inside a Slate callback is swallowed silently): each one carries its reason and is counted as a single warning; there is no `except: pass`
- Black box inputs (plan, limits, name) are validated by hand, not with Pydantic, by the decision to use only the standard library (D68); plan errors are returned and the valid part is recorded
- No `@spec` tag in code or tests: REQ-90 (Store matrix), REQ-95 (headset) and REQ-96 (isolate and compare), which are not in v0.5.0
- The app window (button, dropdown and recording panel) is tested with unit tests and the panel with the user; the editable plan card, the history in the interface and the mark key setting are not there

Texts in es, en and zh-CN. 2 Rust tests and 7 Vitest tests

Tested end to end with a real Unreal recording: list, analysis (2,086 bytes), query and wait. 10 tests plus a manual test against a real session

They do not need the editor. Pending in S109b/c: `session_plan/start/status/mark/stop` and publishing them on both surfaces. 10 tests

The slow-editor warning (R5) now judges a moving window and skips the first 2 s of PIE startup
- **Black box, relation and event analyses (REQ-93, S108b):** `follow` (A in B's frame using its yaw: mean position, maximum deviation, per-axis and total drift, and whether it follows within a tolerance), `transitions` (movement mode, base and pause, in order, with counts and a cap of 50 listed), `timeline` (a summary every N seconds: FPS, speed, transitions and marks) and pause exclusion, which removes frames with PIE paused from the analysis. This closes the S108 core analysis. 11 tests (seeded 0.5 cm/s drift, a rigid follower in a turning frame, pauses never resumed)
- **Black box, movement and measurement analyses (REQ-93, S108a):** `src-tauri/src/sessions/analysis/`. `gaps` (low FPS, hitches, pauses: says whether the rest of the analysis is reliable), `frame_budget` (median, p99 and spikes), `stillness` (still windows and jitter at rest), `oscillation` (two-value loops with period and amplitude) and `jumps` (jumps grouped into episodes with a duration, and whether they come back). Each result says which columns it used and how many samples, and no increment crosses a missing sample or a pause. 23 tests with synthetic sessions with seeded problems (1.7 cm period-2 wobble, a 31 cm blip of 0.18 s, jitter, 3 FPS) and clean ones
- **Black box, storage and reader in Rust (REQ-91, S107):** `src-tauri/src/sessions/`. Reads `frames.bin` (CRC blocks, no new dependency), `meta.json` and `events.jsonl` from `Saved/DBVUnrealAI/Sessions/` inside the app, so it works with the editor closed or hung. A cut or corrupt block, a half-written event line or a broken `meta.json` are dropped and the rest is read; a session the editor never closed is listed as incomplete. Newest-first history, pruning to the last N sessions and a delete that requires confirmation, rejects path-like ids and leaves a recording in progress alone. 12 tests, one with a `frames.bin` generated by the Python recorder (cross-language contract)
- **Black box, real Unreal adapter (REQ-91, S106d):** `dbv_unreal_ai/session/live.py` (`start_recording`, `status`, `mark`, `stop_recording`). Reads actors by player, camera, label or class; sockets; Blueprint variables by reflection (tries the name with spaces and in snake_case); animation instance properties; and horizontal speed. A recording can be armed before pressing Play. It retries missing targets every 0.25 s, flushes to disk at least every second and warns once if the editor is very slow (R5). Verified live in PIE: it detects the end, a missing variable and an absent actor are turned off with a single warning, and the mark lands in `events.jsonl`. +3 tests
- **Black box, recorder core (REQ-91, S106c):** `dbv_unreal_ai/session/recorder.py`, with no `unreal` dependency (the engine comes in through an adapter). It checks the game world is still alive before reading any object and closes when PIE ends, on time or on size; a failure turns off only that signal with a single warning; it retries actors that appear late for 5 s; one active session and exclusion with the playtest; it saves `meta.json` with the measured cost, slow frames and disabled signals, and `events.jsonl` with the marks. 13 tests against a simulated world
- **Black box, recording plan (REQ-91, S106b):** `dbv_unreal_ai/session/plan.py`. Signals (transform, variable, animation property, frame), presets `player`, `player_vr`, `performance`, `input` and `gameplay_events`, and limits (10 min, 500 MB, 20 sessions). A plan with errors is not discarded: the bad parts are returned in `errors` and the good ones are recorded; warning above 30 columns (measured cost, P-22). 9 tests
- **Black box, `frames.bin` format (REQ-91, S106):** `dbv_unreal_ai/session/frames.py`. Self-contained blocks with a `DBVB` header, `f64` times, `f32` columns and a CRC; a cut or corrupt block is dropped and earlier ones still read. Standard library only. 6 tests
- **Drape for tops and trousers (REQ-98):** the shell no longer copies the anatomy. For each height band it takes the convex hull of the body (bridging the sternum and the spine) and lets it hang: going down, the fabric only moves 0.1 cm closer per cm on a top. It only moves away from the skin, never into it. On Pablo's tank top the median clearance goes from 0.98 to 2.93 cm and the abs no longer show.
- **Tops and trousers built from the character's own body (REQ-98):** `outfit/shell.py`. The Tripo garment is no longer deformed: it is placed on the full body with a single scale and position, decides which skin it covers (armholes, neckline and length come from the garment; the crotch is always covered; the arm stays out if it has no sleeves), and the garment is that skin lifted 1 cm (top) or 0.6 cm (trousers), so the top sits outside at the waist. Colour and metal/roughness are baked from Tripo; the weights are the body's. It is the path used by "Send to Unreal" in the Library and by the pipeline for `Torso` and `Legs` (skirts keep their own path). On `Pablo` with the tank top and running shorts: 0 vertices inside the skin and about 6 s per garment.
- **Outfit evals (REQ-88):** new tasks in `scripts/eval/unreal-eval.mjs` checking that the AI uses the deterministic tool, asks when the target is ambiguous and infers the garment kind. The verification matrix (REQ-90) is still pending.
- **Top welding, two-sided materials, and 4 LODs (REQ-81):** new `weld_mesh_vertices` and `weld_top_dynamic_mesh` functions in `dbv_unreal_ai.outfit.fit` to safely weld mesh borders without collapsing open anatomical boundaries; `apply_two_sided_materials` to enforce `override_two_sided=True` and `two_sided=True` across material instances and base assets eliminating invisible backs; and `setup_garment_lods` to procedurally regenerate 4 LOD levels using `SkeletalMeshEditorSubsystem.regenerate_lod` synchronized with the MetaHuman body.
- **Garment validation (REQ-89):** `dbv_unreal_ai.outfit.validate` module with open-border count, leader pose linkage, a PIE walk test and captures. **Real clearance** (`outfit/clearance.py`): signed distance from every garment vertex to the full body of its build, counting only the skin the actor draws, plus bare skin inside the garment area found by casting rays from the skin. If it cannot be measured, the result is `error` with the reason. **Real captures:** front, side and back rendered on the spot with a temporary `SceneCapture2D`, showing only the character, in `Saved/DBVUnrealAI/outfits/captures/<actor>/`. Cloth skirts and dresses are not measured per vertex yet and come out as `error` until they are.
- **Single tool and deterministic pipeline to dress MetaHumans (REQ-88):** new `dbv_unreal_ai.outfit.pipeline` module unifying the entire dressing workflow into a stateful 5-step pipeline (`prepare_character`, `apply_slot_rules`, `prepare_skin`, `fit_and_equip`, `finalize`) with `dress_metahuman_start`, `dress_metahuman_step`, `dress_metahuman_status` and synchronous runner `dress_metahuman`. Persists state in `Saved/DBVUnrealAI/outfits/<job_id>.json`, enforces the single active job constraint per editor (D65), and rejects execution during active PIE sessions. Uniformly exposed across `editor.py`, `toolset.py` (`@toolset_registry.tool_call`), `cpp_bridge.py`, native Rust MCP server (`unreal_dress_metahuman`), built-in assistant (`unrealTools.js`), and "Send to Unreal" (`sendToUnreal.js`), with explicit instructions in the system prompt (`unrealPrompt`) prohibiting manual re-implementation via `run_python` while the single tool exists.
- **Automatic Chaos Cloth physics for skirts and dresses (REQ-87):** new `dbv_unreal_ai.outfit.cloth` module implementing procedural cloth simulation in Unreal Engine 5.8. Generates low-poly simulation meshes using `GeometryScript_MeshSimplification`, creates and configures `ChaosClothAsset` and `Dataflow` assets without modal dialogs by binding `DataflowInstance.dataflow_asset`, adds `ChaosClothComponent` to the character's Blueprint via `SubobjectDataSubsystem`, and sets up `SetLeaderPoseComponent` to `Body` in `EventBeginPlay` using `BlueprintTools` DSL.
- **Choosing who gets dressed (REQ-83):** the app no longer equips a garment on "the first MetaHuman in the level". The Library asks who to dress (with one it comes preselected, with several you must pick and with none the garment is only imported), the AI calls `unreal_list_metahumans` and asks when there are several, `library_send_to_unreal` and `unreal_equip_metahuman_outfit` take the target (`metahuman`, `actor_label`) and, without one, they neither equip nor place anything: the garment is only imported and the user is told. If the editor cannot equip it, the error comes back as is instead of leaving the garment loose in front of the camera.
- **Garment types and cut (REQ-84):** Generate offers `top`, `pants`, `skirt`, `dress` and `shoes` instead of the slot, plus a female, male or neutral cut. Each type has its own prompt (a dress is a whole garment without a body) and the cut goes into it. The type and cut are saved in the library and, when equipping, a warning is shown if the cut does not suit the chosen body. Old entries and calls with only `garment_slot` keep working and get their type.
- **"Prepare" enables the plugins for dressing (REQ-87):** `GeometryScripting`, `ChaosClothAssetToolset` and `PythonFoundationPackages` (`numpy` and `Pillow`; the first start needs a network connection), only the ones the project's engine ships, as "Editor only" when the app adds them, with their check and explanation in the diagnosis.

- **Debugging a runtime failure (REQ-97, first step of the black box):** new *Diagnose a runtime failure* recipe and rules in the AI prompt: when a failure happens during play, measure before changing code; a memory note or a comment is a hypothesis, not a fact; say what was measured and what was not; and never record with per-frame callbacks in `unreal_run_python` (on 2026-11-08 one flooded the log with tens of thousands of lines). If the model writes one in its reply, the app reminds it.

### Fixed
- **Security, Critical (`/code-simplify` review): a session name could escape its folder (REQ-91, REQ-92):** `session_start` put the `name` parameter (written by the AI) as is into the folder id, so a name like `../../x` wrote `frames.bin`, `meta.json` and `events.jsonl` outside `Saved/DBVUnrealAI/Sessions/`. The label now only allows letters, numbers, `_` and `-` (40 at most), cleaned in Rust and again in Python, with hostile-name tests and checked live (`../../x y` → `x-y`)
- **A recording whose start failed halfway stayed hooked (REQ-91):** the session stayed "active" (blocking the next ones), with files open and the editor setting not restored. It now closes what it opened and re-raises the error
- **The mark key and the movement events were turned off if the character did not exist yet (REQ-91):** in the first frames of Play the controller or the character may not have appeared, and that turned the signal off for the whole session (marks with F10 might not work depending on timing). It now retries without turning anything off
- **"Finish" also stopped a Play session the recorder did not start (REQ-92):** in "Session already running" mode the session belongs to the person. `session_stop` only stops Play if the recorder started it (pie mode)
- **A plan cannot ask to record forever or fill the disk (REQ-91):** hard caps of 1 hour, 2 GB and 200 sessions (with a warning), on top of the defaults of 10 minutes, 500 MB and 20 sessions
- Compliance: the `dataclass`es in `session/plan.py` become `slots=True`
- **A useless warning in the log in every session (REQ-91):** the `gameplay_events` preset promised `log_error`, which does not exist yet, and the recorder warned about it at the start of every session (50 identical lines in 50 sessions). It is removed from the preset until it exists
- **`unreal_playtest` asked for the log with arguments that do not exist (REQ-82):** `GetLogEntries` requires `category`, `pattern` and `maxEntries` and the playtest sent other names; the log report came back empty without a warning, as happened with `StartPIE`. Fixed in Rust and JavaScript
- **The editor kept using the old recorder after the plugin was updated (REQ-91):** the editor's Python interpreter keeps modules in memory until it restarts, so with the key already changed to F10 the recorder kept listening for F9 (the user pressed F10 and no mark appeared; the AI talked about F9). `session_start` now imports the recorder as it is on disk, unless a recording is in progress (so it is neither lost nor a second one started); `status`, `mark` and `stop` do not reload. Checked live: with F9 still in memory, the new start gave `mark_key` F10. There is a test. For the tool texts the app has to be restarted (the Rust binary carries them)
- **The default mark key changes from F9 to F10 (REQ-94):** F9 is the user's screenshot key, and pressing it to mark also triggered the capture, which caused a small pause that the analysis detected (the mark and the hitch coincided because the same key press caused both). It changes in the plan, the recorder, the panel, the tool texts and the three languages; it is still configurable with `mark_key`
- **Losing editor focus while using the black box left the game at ~3 FPS with no keyboard (REQ-91, REQ-94):** in the second test, clicking the app panel took focus from Unreal, which throttled itself because of "Use less CPU when in background" and the character got no keyboard, so there was no movement to record. The recorder now removes that throttle while recording (in memory only, without saving the settings) and always restores it when the session closes; it is noted in `meta.json`. Checked live by taking the editor's focus for 9 s with another window: it stayed between 72 and 81 FPS for the whole 34 s recording (before ~3) and the setting went back to its value
- **Marking without losing focus (REQ-94):** a new in-game mark key, **F9** by default (configurable with `mark_key` in the plan), which adds a numbered mark just like the panel, edge-triggered (holding it does not repeat). The app panel still has "Mark moment" and "Finish", but clicking it takes the keyboard away from the game; the AI explains it and asks for one click inside the game at the start. Not checked with a real key press (only with the simulator and by calling the engine API)
- **The recording panel read `startedAt` but `started_at` arrived (REQ-94):** the clock worked through a fallback, but did not use the real start time. `ActiveSession` now goes out in camelCase, with the mark key
- **`gaps` called a session with 10 s at 3 FPS reliable (REQ-93):** it counted frames, not time; in a 55 s recording at ~90 FPS those 10 s are about 30 frames, under 1 %. It now also measures the fraction of TIME spent in slow frames and detects stretches of low FPS lasting 1 s or more (where they start and at how many FPS), and reports them as a problem. The first test with the assistant found it: the AI said "reliable" and "the editor stalled" at once (+1 test with that case)
- **The AI asked the user to type "now" or "done" in a blocked chat (REQ-94):** while recording, its turn occupies the conversation and the Send button is unavailable, so the person could not mark or finish. The tool texts, the prompt, the recipe and the three button modes no longer ask for it: they point to the app's recording panel ("Mark moment", "Finish") and say `session_wait` must be repeated until it ends
- **"Black box" dropdown unreadable in the sepia theme (REQ-94):** it used a variable that does not exist (`--surface`) with a dark fallback and the text inherited the theme colour, so it read dark on dark. It now uses the theme colours (`--bg-secondary`, `--text-primary`, `--border`, `--bg-tertiary`, `--shadow`) and a test stops fixed colours coming back. The user saw it in the first test
- **The built-in assistant could not start Play in `unreal_playtest` either (REQ-82):** the JavaScript copy sent the same argument UE 5.8.2 rejects. It now uses `options` and a test checks it. Also, a comment in `metahuman.py` with a character name broke the REQ-83 hygiene test, and the Python recorder code (`session/`) broke the single-`return` rule of `dbv-lint`: refactored without changing behaviour (checked live again)
- **`unreal_playtest` could not start Play on UE 5.8.2 (REQ-82):** it sent `{"bSimulateInEditor": false}` to `StartPIE`, but the real schema requires `options` (`bSimulate`, `playMode`, `warmupSeconds`) and rejects the call with "input param \"options\" is required". The tests accepted it because the simulated editor takes any argument. It now uses the real schema, shared with the black box, and a test checks it
- **Sending the same Tripo model to Unreal again imported nothing:** its textures and materials clashed with the first import ("the import produced no assets"). It now retries in a new subfolder.
- **Assertion crash in `INPAINT_WEIGHTS` (`Map.h.inl:651`) and garment distortion (REQ-81):** fixed handling of sparse vertex identifiers (*tombstones*) produced by `weld_mesh_edges` in `GeometryScript`. `GeometryScript_MeshRepair.compact_mesh` is now strictly enforced after edge welding and repair passes, guaranteeing dense `0..N-1` vertex index arrays in `_deform_by_envelopes` and preventing untransformed vertices from remaining anchored at ground level ($Z \in [-50, 0]$), which caused vertical curtain mesh stretching and the null pointer assertion crash in Epic's `TransferBoneWeights.cpp` solver. Added skeleton pre-copy (`copy_bones_from_mesh`) and fallback weight transfer.
- **Validation accepted badly fitted clothes (REQ-89, REJECTED-13):** clearance was estimated by comparing bounding boxes and, on failure, returned a fixed 1.2/2.0 cm; skirts with a trimmed body were reported "without penetration" without measuring; and captures used a path relative to the engine and the asynchronous viewport camera, so they returned paths to files that did not exist. Measured on 2026-11-10: `Pablo_Deportista`'s tank top had 27.8 % of its vertices inside the skin (up to 8.7 cm) and the shorts left 10.2 % of the area bare at the crotch. They now return `error`, and Pablo's factory clothing returns `ok`.
- **Shorts classification and anatomical fitting (REQ-81, REQ-84):** `_is_skirt_shape` now recognizes short/pants keywords (`short`, `pantalon`, `correr`), preventing athletic shorts from being misclassified as a conical skirt with planar waist cuts. Shorts are now procedurally fitted between waist and mid-thigh ($Z \in [75, 103]$) with clean waist overlap with the top.
- DBVUnrealAI plugin: the project would not open after installing it, because it declared a dependency on `GLTFImporter`, which does not exist in UE 5.8.
- MetaHuman clothing (REQ-81): the gap between head and shoulders no longer appears when replacing the stock shirt (`NeckHideScale`, which shrank the neck through the material).
- MetaHuman skirts (REQ-81): straight hem, no open patches, and a tight fit at the hips with a straight drop, without skin poking through the fabric at rest.
- Torso garments (REQ-81): the fit no longer clips the real waist width, which made skin show through.
