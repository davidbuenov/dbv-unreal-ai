# DBV Unreal AI v0.5.1 — Release notes

**[🇪🇸 Español](./v0.5.1.md) · 🇬🇧 English** · 2026-11-12

Version v0.5.1: **cloth dresses end to end** with `unreal_dress_metahuman` (the Tripo dress is fitted to the body, oriented by its silhouette, its sleeves follow the arms and the skirt moves with Chaos Cloth pinned at the bodice; validation with measured clearance), **returning already fitted garments to the Library** to reuse them, the **tailor** for tops and pants, and a batch of fixes to the dressing pipeline that stopped skirts and dresses from being equipped or left leftovers and fake successes.

### Added
- **Return an already fitted garment to the Library (REQ-100):** `unreal_export_outfit` exports the garment a MetaHuman is wearing (FBX plus an `outfit_metadata.json` manifest with body build, slot, meshes and materials) and saves it to the Library; dressing it again on a body of the same build equips it as is, without re-fitting it. `library_save_outfit_from_unreal` command, MCP tool and app AI tool.
- **Garment fitting with the tailor algorithm (REQ-81, REQ-98, ADR-V050-007, S119):** new `dbv_unreal_ai.outfit.tailor` module that fits the original Tripo garment mesh to the MetaHuman body while preserving its original topology, UV mapping, and materials. Uses orientation normalization with neckline/rise detection (`normalise`), anatomical landmark alignment (`landmarks_body`), horizontal inner cap triangle removal (`drop_inner_caps`), inner radius polar clamping over the convex hull (`band_clamp_polar`), signed-distance skin repulsion (`band_clamp_distance`), smoothed bone weight transfer on welded groups (`skin_weights`), and welded normal recomputation (`welded_normals`). Supports optional top length compression (`limit_top_length`, `False` by default) and automatic layering (`under_skm`) to keep tops over pants.
- **Tailor test suite (REQ-81, REQ-98):** new `tests/python/test_outfit_tailor.py` with 7 pure unit tests on synthetic meshes without Unreal dependencies.
- **Tripo dresses are fitted to the body before the cloth is set up (REQ-87, REQ-88):** `unreal_dress_metahuman` with `garment_kind: "dress"` no longer builds the simulation on the mesh as delivered (a 1 m box at the origin, which left the dress as a heap at the character's feet). The new `tailor.fit_dress` scales by chest and waist, moves the armhole to the armpit and the waist to the body's waist, turns each sleeve onto the shoulder-elbow-hand line while keeping its volume, removes the solid's caps that the neck, cuffs and legs pass through (and the mannequin stubs left loose), takes in the bodice and keeps the whole garment at least 1 cm from the skin. `build_cloth_garment` saves the render mesh (with Tripo's UVs and material) and a welded simulation mesh, and computes the per-vertex `MaxDistance` map: bodice and sleeves follow the body, the skirt is free from the hips with 9 cm of travel. Tested in the editor on `Victoria_VestidoAzul`: validation `ok`, 0 % of vertices inside the skin, median clearance 4.7 cm.
- **`flip_orientation` in `unreal_dress_metahuman` (REQ-87, REQ-88):** optional parameter (default `false`) to turn a dress whose front was detected backwards by 180°. It is meant for a retry after looking at the captures.

### Changed
- **A dress's clearance is actually measured (REQ-89):** `validate_outfit` always returned `error` for skirts and dresses ("not yet measured vertex by vertex"), so no cloth garment could finish the pipeline. It now uses the signed distance the fit measured for each vertex against the full body; without those measurements it is still `error`. The Physics Asset check looks at the Cloth Asset, which is where the cloth's collision lives.

### Fixed
- **Dressing skirts and dresses with `unreal_dress_metahuman` (REQ-87, REQ-88):** no `skirt` or `dress` garment could be equipped. The `fit_and_equip` step passed `dest_folder` to a function that did not accept it and, besides, the pipeline read from `prepare_character_copy` a key that does not exist (`copy_blueprint_path` instead of `copy_bp_path`): the copy Blueprint was left empty, the slot rules were not applied and the cloth setup received the actor label as a Blueprint path. Now the copy Blueprint is used, the slot rules are applied on the level actor, a garment on disk is imported and must yield a `StaticMesh`, and the cloth weights are transferred from the character's own body and Physics Asset (before, fixed Victoria paths that do not exist in the project). Not yet tested in the editor.
- **Cloth garments no longer leave leftovers in the root folder (REQ-87, REQ-88):** each skirt or dress is created in its own folder (`<destination>/<name>`) and, if the Cloth Asset cannot be created, the simulation mesh, the Dataflow and the half-built Cloth Asset are deleted.
- **A failed dressing job no longer destroys earlier garments (REQ-85, REQ-88):** the rollback added on 2026-11-11 reverted any copy to the original Blueprint, including a character already dressed by earlier jobs, whose garments live on the level actor. Now only the copy created by the failing job is reverted; on an already dressed copy the slots get back the meshes they had. The response includes `rollback` with what was done.
- **`unreal_export_outfit` no longer saves fake garments (REQ-100):** if the FBX was not exported, an OBJ with a filler triangle was written and success was returned. Now it fails with the reason and leaves nothing behind. The garment name is sanitised before it is used as a file name and the `Feet` slot is recorded as `shoes`.
- **A garment tailored for another body build is rejected with a clear message (REQ-81, REQ-100):** the path that claimed to re-fit it with the tailor could not work (the tailor only reads a `StaticMesh`).
- **Equipping by bones no longer returns a simulated success (REQ-88):** an exception while equipping answered `success: true` with a made-up mesh path.
- **The Library only imports folders with `outfit_metadata.json` (REQ-100):** `import_tailored_outfit` copied the files of any folder.
- **[Important, `/code-simplify` review] A dress whose cloth setup fails leaves no loose meshes (REQ-87, REQ-88):** the render and simulation meshes created by the fit stayed in the garment folder if the Cloth Asset then failed. They are now deleted with the rest of the attempt.
- **The dress keeps its colour (REQ-87):** a reused cloth component kept the previous garment's material (a blue dress came out burgundy) and the Cloth Asset does not carry Tripo's. The override materials are now cleared and the garment's material is assigned.
- **Shirt and jeans no longer reappear under the dress (REQ-84):** compiling the copy's Blueprint rebuilds the actor and put the clothes back into the slots that `apply_slot_rules` had emptied. They are removed again after the cloth is set up.
- **Validation captures show the whole character (REQ-89):** a freshly replaced actor has no pose until the next frame and was captured collapsed on the floor (only the feet were visible). The pose of body, clothes and cloth is now recomputed before capturing.
- **Dresses are no longer put on back to front (REQ-87):** the front was decided by the neckline, as for a top, and a dress with ruffles at the neck or a low back came out turned 180° (the seat at the front). It is now decided by the silhouette: the back is the side where the waist curves in between the upper back and the seat. The neckline only decides when the silhouette cannot tell (`docs/2026-11-12-dress-metahuman-neckline-orientation-flip.md`).
- **A narrow bodice is no longer mistaken for sleeves (REQ-87):** a band with few points in the centre (a sparse inner cap) was taken for a bodice with sleeves on each side and blew up the scale. And the arm bone, which runs diagonally, no longer marks the wall of a sleeveless dress as a cap.

### Known
- **Clean skin and body trimming do not run in `unreal_dress_metahuman` (REQ-86):** the `prepare_skin` step passed them the actor instead of its label, so they never did anything. It is now explicitly disabled until a decision is made: trimming uses `create_new_skeletal_mesh_asset_from_mesh`, which scrambles the bone order in UE 5.8.
- **Skirts are not fitted yet (REQ-87):** they are set up on the mesh as delivered. Only dresses (`dress`) are fitted to the body.
- **A dress without a clear lower-back curve is oriented by its neckline:** if the silhouette cannot tell front from back, it may come out turned; fix it with `flip_orientation: true`.
- **Hair may be missing from the validation captures:** recomputing the pose leaves the groom undrawn in that capture; it shows normally in the viewport and in Play.
- **The plugin cloth templates (`Content/Cloth`) are copies of the test project's:** they keep references to `/Game/DBV/Runway/Cloth/...` and to the `f_med_nrw` body. The code replaces them when it creates each garment, but in another project they will load with missing-reference warnings.

### Removed
- **Body shell method (REJECTED-15):** removed `unreal-plugin/DBVUnrealAI/Content/Python/dbv_unreal_ai/outfit/shell.py` and `tests/python/test_outfit_shell.py`, replaced entirely by the tailor algorithm.
